# WoW Forever — "My honest beta review, and my biggest fears" (script complet v5)

Durée visée : ~14 min (≈ 2 046 mots de narration). Langue : anglais.
[Entre crochets : ce qu'on voit à l'écran.]
Partie 1 = bilan de la bêta (ce que j'ai vécu). Partie 2 = pourquoi Forever existe (mon analyse).
Partie 3 = mes 5 craintes pour l'avenir. Partie 4 = ce que Forever aurait dû être. Partie 5 = Forever décidera de l'avenir de WoW (3 scénarios + ma prédiction ironique pour retail).

---

## 0. HOOK (0:00 – 0:20)

[Plan de la bêta en jeu, gros titre : "MY HONEST BETA REVIEW"]

The WoW Forever beta is almost over, and the game launches in three weeks.
I played it a lot, and I dug into the game files. So here's my honest review: what I loved, what went wrong,
why I think this game was rushed, and my biggest fears for its future. Because Blizzard sold it as Classic Plus. And it's not.

---

## PART 1 — MY BETA REVIEW

### 1.1 What I loved (0:20 – 1:20)

[Carte : "WHAT I LOVED"]

First, the good news.

[Carte : "MORE DEPTH THAN CLASSIC"]

The characters have more depth. More gameplay options, more theorycrafting around your character.
Maybe my memories are wrong, but it feels richer than Classic ever was.

[Plans : brouillard, lumière, eau]

And the new lighting and fog change everything. The same old zones suddenly look beautiful.

### 1.2 The new dungeons (1:20 – 2:05)

[Carte : "3 NEW DUNGEONS" — Ruins of Lordaeron · Excavation Site · City of Dalaran]

I ran three of the new dungeons: the Ruins of Lordaeron, the Excavation Site in the Wetlands, and the City of Dalaran.
All three are really good.

[Carte : "A FEW NEW IDEAS"]

Some bosses have a few more mechanics than in Classic. Nothing revolutionary, but it's a nice touch.

[Carte : "NOW DO IT FOR THE OLD ONES"]

My only regret: why not give the old Classic dungeons a light mechanical rework too? Same dungeons, same bosses, just a bit more interesting to fight.

### 1.3 What went wrong (2:05 – 3:20)

[Carte : "WHAT WENT WRONG"]

Now the problems. And most of them are not the game itself. It's the players.

[Carte : "NEW PLAYERS ARE NOT WELCOME"]

A friend of mine had never played WoW before. He started on the beta. It wasn't a nightmare, but some veteran players are really hard on beginners. Not patient, not open.
If Forever wants new players, that has to change.

[Carte : "THE MEGASERVER"]

Then the megaserver. On paper, I loved the idea. In practice, it's very toxic.
Americans against Europeans, and everything turns political in seconds. Everyone attacks everyone for their opinion.
I'm not taking any side here. But it goes way too far, and Blizzard should enforce the chat rules much harder.

[Carte : "MY IDEA: PROXIMITY CHAT"]

And here's an idea that could help a lot, especially for new players: proximity chat.
Talk to the people around you, in the world, in your dungeon.
Turn it off in capital cities, or it becomes chaos. And rework the in-game voice chat while you're at it.
This game is made for it.

[Carte : "PVP: EMPTY QUEUES"]

PvP? I wanted to try the new battleground. I queued, and nobody was there. So I gave up.

## PART 2 — WHY DOES FOREVER EXIST? (3:20 – 5:00)

[Carte : "MY THEORY: A RESCUE PLAN"]

Now, my theory. And this is my opinion.
I think WoW Forever, and honestly the whole last BlizzCon, were rescue announcements.

[Carte : "BLIZZCON 2026" — "StarCraft shooter: 2030 · Diablo V: 2029 · The Last Titan: 2027"]

Look at what they showed: a StarCraft game for 2030. Diablo 5 for 2029. A tiny teaser for The Last Titan.
Games that are years away. To me, that's not a show for players. That's a show to reassure investors.

[Carte : "TURTLE WOW: SHUT DOWN BY BLIZZARD (APRIL 2026)"]

Then look at the timing. Turtle WoW, a huge fan-made Classic Plus server, with new zones and new races, was taken down by Blizzard in court this year.
Players clearly wanted Classic Plus. Blizzard saw the numbers.

[Carte : "CATACLYSM (DEC 2010): 12M+ SUBSCRIBERS · 3.3M COPIES IN 24H" — "MIDNIGHT: ~500K ONLINE IN WEEK 1 (ESTIMATE)"]

And on retail, Midnight is struggling. Its story is getting destroyed by the players.
Some sites celebrated half a million players online in its first week. Half a million. When Cataclysm launched, World of Warcraft had more than twelve million subscribers, and Cataclysm sold 3.3 million copies in its first 24 hours.
That's not a success. That's a shadow of what this game used to be.

[Carte : "IN THE BETA FILES" — "271 Season of Discovery rune spells · 104 Waylaid Supplies · Scarlet Enclave"]

So I think Forever was meant to come later, maybe after the Worldsoul Saga, and it was pushed out in a hurry.
And the files back that up. I dug into the beta client: it is full of Season of Discovery leftovers.
271 rune engraving spells. Over a hundred Waylaid Supplies items. The Scarlet Enclave map.

[Carte : "BUILT ON SEASON OF DISCOVERY"]

To me, they took the Season of Discovery client and servers, and reworked them.
I honestly doubt there's more than six months of real work on this beta. And six months might be generous.

[Carte : "THERE WAS A MARKET TO TAKE"]

So why does Forever exist? Simple. There was a market to take.
Private servers were taking it. Blizzard shut them down. And now Blizzard takes that market.

[Carte : "WITH THIS BUDGET?"]

And some people will say: reusing code is normal. Sure, a little. But at this scale, with Blizzard's budget? To me, that's crazy.

[Carte : "DIABLO ANIMATED SERIES · NETFLIX"]

I'll go even further. I think Blizzard knows its future in video games is uncertain.
At BlizzCon, they announced an animated Diablo series with Netflix. I think they're going to diversify: shows, productions, things like that.
Because if they keep going like this with their games, in my opinion, they could be dead.

## PART 3 — MY 5 BIGGEST FEARS

### Fear 1 — It's a remake, not Classic+

[Carte : "FEAR #1 — AN IDENTITY CRISIS"]

Let's be honest. Forever is not Classic Plus. It's a remake of World of Warcraft.
New zones, new dungeons, new races, new systems, upgraded graphics. That's a new game built on the old world.

And selling it as Classic Plus is a mistake. Classic purists hear "Classic", and they want nothing touched. Every change becomes a fight.
So Blizzard gives a little to the purists, then a little to the players who want innovation, and nobody knows what this game really wants to be.

[Carte : "KEEP THE ART STYLE. UPGRADE THE TEXTURES."]

Look at the graphics: gorgeous water and lighting, and textures from 2004.
I'm not asking for a new art direction. The original Warcraft style is perfect. Retail's problem was never texture quality, it's the cartoonish, almost Disney style.
Forever could have kept the original style and rebuilt it properly. A true remake.

### Fear 2 — The money

[Carte : "FEAR #2 — PAY TO UNLOCK" — "SKYBORNE: $29.99"]

Blizzard talks about seasons for the endgame. And here's a fact: the new Skyborne race is already sold separately. Your subscription is not enough, you need the Heroic Pack. Thirty dollars.

So what about the next race? The next class? Thirty euros here, fifty euros there?

[Carte : "TODAY: no battle pass · no WoW Token · no paid boosts"]

To be fair, right now Blizzard says: no battle pass, no WoW Token, no paid boosts. Right now.
But a paid race on day one is a precedent. On top of a subscription.

[Carte : "MY PREDICTION: EVERY NEW RACE AND CLASS WILL BE PAID"]

And I'll make a prediction. I know Blizzard. With these seasons, they're going to make a ton of money.
Every new race will be paid. Every new class too, probably. Maybe free after a year or so. But let's not kid ourselves:
a game like this, with no expansions, will not live without paid content. I don't believe it for a second.
It would be sad to see WoW go that way.

[Carte : "SKYBORNE = BLOOD ELF SKELETON" — fichiers du jeu : même squelette (FDID 4690403 / 4690402), armure, textures et casques en repli sur l'elfe de sang]

And let's talk about that paid race. I actually like the Skyborne idea. Air elves, the lore of Eldre'Thalas, the Highborne. I love it.
But the design? I checked the game files. The Skyborne are built on the blood elf skeleton: same bones, same animations, same silhouette.
And for armor, textures and helmets, the game falls back on the blood elf. Exactly like the void elves in retail.
New paint, same body. It's a blue blood elf. A high elf, recolored.

[Carte : "AT LEAST REWORK THE NIGHTBORNE"]

Blizzard is the champion of recycling, I know. But at least take the Nightborne models from retail and rework them into something new.
Thirty dollars for a recolor is hard to swallow.

### Fear 3 — Too fast

[Carte : "FEAR #3 — THE PACE" — "Dec 9 · Winter · Spring · Summer"]

Raids in December, Hardcore in winter, a big update in spring, another one in summer. A huge endgame block every three months or so.
That feels fast. Very fast. Some players need that rhythm, I get it.
But instead of giant blocks, why not smaller, really interesting content in between?

### Fear 4 — Playing it too safe

[Carte : "FEAR #4 — BE BRAVE" — "no bleed on elementals · no bleed on machines"]

Since it's not Classic, take the best of twenty years of WoW. Cataclysm had great gameplay ideas. Push class identity, and make every spec feel unique.
Without losing the Classic logic I love: you can't make a ghost or a machine bleed. That's Warcraft.
For me, Forever should have been WoW 2.

### Fear 5 — The Disneyfication

[Carte : "FEAR #5 — WARCRAFT IS DARK"]

Warcraft was born from war. Power struggles, ego wars, hatred between races. A dark fantasy world.

[Carte : "RETAIL TODAY" — Liadrin, patch 12.1.5]

Look at retail right now: Lady Liadrin dies in patch 12.1.5, and players are furious. Not because she died, but because it was rushed, with no weight.
Most of the veteran developers are gone, and the new vision is different from the players who built this game.

[Carte : "METZEN IS BACK... REALLY?"]

Remember the Worldsoul Saga announcement? Chris Metzen was back, and everyone thought he would save WoW.
In my opinion, he was brought back for his name and his image. I don't see his touch in the game today.
Yet look at his tabletop games at Warchief Gaming: that's his style, clearly.

[Carte : '"I WISH WE HADN\'T CALLED IT WARCRAFT. IT SOUNDS INTIMIDATING."' — relayed by Holly Longdale, 2026]

And then Holly Longdale says that Metzen sometimes wishes they hadn't called it Warcraft, because it sounds intimidating.
Really? The man who helped write Garrosh Hellscream, one of the most brutal warlords in Warcraft? I find that very hard to believe. Unless he said it in a sarcastic way.

[Carte : "WARCRAFT MUST STAY WARCRAFT"]

The problem is that everything became political. I'm not afraid to say it: Blizzard needs a reset.
A neutral creative direction, built on what Warcraft is. Not everyone in this world is a big tough warrior, and that's fine, it takes everyone to make a world.
But if some people don't like the identity of Warcraft, they can make another game, for that audience. Overwatch is a good example: an original title that fits that other audience much better. And I don't mind, Overwatch is Overwatch.
But Warcraft must stay Warcraft. It must not become Disney.
And I can feel that softer tone slowly coming into Forever too.

## PART 4 — WHAT FOREVER SHOULD HAVE BEEN

[Carte : "WE'RE SETTLING FOR CRUMBS"]

Let's go further. With Blizzard's resources, couldn't they have gone much further in rebuilding Azeroth?
This game deserved a new graphics engine. Way more new content. New quests, new stories, everywhere.
The new dungeons are cool, yes. But honestly? We're settling for crumbs.

[Carte : "IT SHOULD BE DELAYED BY A YEAR"]

If we're realistic, Forever should be delayed by a year, to be rebuilt properly.
But retail is in such a bad place, and we're so nostalgic for the old World of Warcraft, that we accept crumbs. That's where we are. It's sad to say.
And I know it's too late. It will never be delayed.

[Carte : "A BRAND NEW GAME, WITH AN OLD SOUL"]

But I know what would have worked for everyone. Even the people who think they wouldn't like it on paper would have loved it in game:
A completely new game. Old school mechanics. Rooted in the lore.
Less "you are the hero, the chosen legend". More adventure in the world.

[Carte : "THE KEY OF WORLD OF WARCRAFT IS THE WORLD"]

Because the key of World of Warcraft is the world.
And the conflict between the Horde and the Alliance. Stop making them best friends every time a new threat shows up.
Sometimes a threat is huge for one faction and minor for the other. And that's fine. That's war. That's Warcraft.

[Carte : "NOT A BAD GAME. A MINIMUM EFFORT GAME."]

Forever is not a bad game. But Blizzard can do so much better, if they gave themselves the means.
They won't. They'll do the bare minimum for maximum profit. Let's not kid ourselves.

---

## PART 5 — FOREVER WILL DECIDE THE FUTURE OF WOW

[Carte : "THE NEXT 2 YEARS DECIDE EVERYTHING"]

And here's the thing people don't realize. I think the future of World of Warcraft, as a franchise, depends on Forever more than anything else.
Not life or death. But what Blizzard decides to do with WoW. And I see three scenarios.

[Carte : "SCENARIO 1 — BIG HYPE, FAST FADE"]

Scenario one: huge hype at launch, then it runs out of breath fast. A bit like Classic did.
Then Blizzard won't bother. They'll keep the usual formula. The retail formula.

[Carte : "SCENARIO 2 — FOREVER BECOMES THE MAIN WOW"]

Scenario two: Forever holds strong, all the way, for two years.
Then I think Forever becomes the main World of Warcraft.

[Carte : "SCENARIO 3 — WOW 2"]

Scenario three: players love a lot of what Forever does, but the "it must stay Classic" side holds back a lot of people.
Then I think Blizzard makes a new game. The sequel to retail, taking all the good ideas from Forever, in a modern way. A WoW 2.

[Carte : "BLIZZARD IS TESTING THE WATERS"]

From a pure marketing point of view, that's exactly what they're measuring right now. Forever is a test.

[Carte : "AND RETAIL? WE KNOW HOW IT ENDS"]

And retail? Honestly, I wouldn't be surprised if it ends like this.
Xal'atath fails to merge with Azeroth, so someone else does it instead. Out of nowhere. Azshara comes back. Maybe Illidan, who knows. Nothing shocks me anymore.
Then the Titans show up, unhappy, because Azeroth's energy was supposed to feed their plans. Big scandal, a little fight.
And in the end, we free Sargeras, because we "understand his point of view". Plot twist: the villain wasn't the villain. Very original, as usual.
Everybody merges with Azeroth, the energy is shared with everyone, and everything goes back to normal.

[Carte : "WOW 2: 30 TO 40 YEARS LATER?"]

And if there's a WoW 2, I bet it takes place thirty or forty years later. Don't look any further. That's how it's going to be.

## OUTRO

[Carte : "BLIZZARD, JUST SAY IT"]

So Blizzard, be clear. It's not Classic. It will never be Classic. It's a remake, a new start. Sell it like that, and build it like that.

[Carte : "LOOKING FOR A GUILD"]

And one last thing: I really want to clear the endgame at launch. But for that, I need a guild. Join one, or build my own.
So if you're starting fresh too, tell me in the comments. Maybe we build it together.

[Carte : "AM I WRONG?"]

Am I wrong about all this? Tell me. And follow for every WoW Forever update.
