# Revised narration

## Scene 01

The World of Warcraft Forever beta is almost over, and the game launches in three weeks. I played it a lot, and I dug into the game files. So here's my honest review: what I loved, what went wrong, why I think this game was rushed, and my biggest fears for its future. Because Blizzard sold it as Classic Plus. And it's not.

## Scene 02

First, the good news.

## Scene 03

The characters have more depth. More gameplay options, more theorycrafting around your character. Maybe my memories are wrong, but it feels richer than Classic ever was.

## Scene 04

And the new lighting and fog change everything. The same old zones suddenly look beautiful.

## Scene 05

I ran three of the new dungeons: the Ruins of Lordaeron, the Excavation Site in the Wetlands, and the City of Dalaran. All three are really good.

## Scene 06

Some bosses have a few more mechanics than in Classic. Nothing revolutionary, but it's a nice touch.

## Scene 07

My only regret: why not give the old Classic dungeons a light mechanical rework too? Same dungeons, same bosses, just a bit more interesting to fight.

## Scene 08

Now the problems. And most of them are not the game itself. It's the players.

## Scene 09

A friend of mine had never played World of Warcraft before. He started on the beta. It wasn't a nightmare, but some veteran players are really hard on beginners. Not patient, not open. If Forever wants new players, that has to change.

## Scene 10

Then the megaserver. On paper, I loved the idea. In practice, it's very toxic. Americans against Europeans, and everything turns political in seconds. Everyone attacks everyone for their opinion. I'm not taking any side here. But it goes way too far, and Blizzard should enforce the chat rules much harder.

## Scene 11

And here's an idea that could help a lot, especially for new players: proximity chat. Talk to the people around you, in the world, in your dungeon. Turn it off in capital cities, or it becomes chaos. And rework the in-game voice chat while you're at it. This game is made for it.

## Scene 12

PvP? I wanted to try the new battleground. I queued, and nobody was there. So I gave up.

## Scene 13

Now, my theory. And this is my opinion. I think World of Warcraft Forever, and honestly the whole last BlizzCon, were rescue announcements.

## Scene 14

Look at what they showed: a StarCraft game for 2030. Diablo 5 for 2029. A tiny teaser for The Last Titan. Games that are years away. To me, that's not a show for players. That's a show to reassure investors.

## Scene 15

Then look at the timing. Turtle Wow, a huge fan-made Classic Plus server, with new zones and new races, was taken down by Blizzard in court this year. Players clearly wanted Classic Plus. Blizzard saw the numbers.

## Scene 16

And on retail, Midnight is struggling. Its story is getting destroyed by the players. Some sites celebrated half a million players online in its first week. Half a million. When Cataclysm launched, World of Warcraft had more than twelve million subscribers, and Cataclysm sold 3.3 million copies in its first 24 hours. That's not a success. That's a shadow of what this game used to be.

## Scene 17

So I think Forever was meant to come later, maybe after the Worldsoul Saga, and it was pushed out in a hurry. And the files back that up. I dug into the beta client: it is full of Season of Discovery leftovers. 271 rune engraving spells. Over a hundred Waylaid Supplies items. The Scarlet Enclave map.

## Scene 18

To me, they took the Season of Discovery client and servers, and reworked them. I honestly doubt there's more than six months of real work on this beta. And six months might be generous.

## Scene 19

So why does Forever exist? Simple. There was a market to take. Private servers were taking it. Blizzard shut them down. And now Blizzard takes that market.

## Scene 20

And some people will say: reusing code is normal. Sure, a little. But at this scale, with Blizzard's budget? To me, that's crazy.

## Scene 21

I'll go even further. I think Blizzard knows its future in video games is uncertain. At BlizzCon, they announced an animated Diablo series with Netflix. I think they're going to diversify: shows, productions, things like that. Because if they keep going like this with their games, in my opinion, they could be dead.

## Scene 22

Let's be honest. Forever is not Classic Plus. It's a remake of World of Warcraft. New zones, new dungeons, new races, new systems, upgraded graphics. That's a new game built on the old world. And selling it as Classic Plus is a mistake. Classic purists hear "Classic", and they want nothing touched. Every change becomes a fight. So Blizzard gives a little to the purists, then a little to the players who want innovation, and nobody knows what this game really wants to be.

## Scene 23

Look at the graphics: gorgeous water and lighting, and textures from 2004. I'm not asking for a new art direction. The original Warcraft style is perfect. Retail's problem was never texture quality, it's the cartoonish, almost Disney style. Forever could have kept the original style and rebuilt it properly. A true remake.

## Scene 24

Blizzard talks about seasons for the endgame. And here's a fact: the new Skyborne race is already sold separately. Your subscription is not enough, you need the Heroic Pack. Thirty dollars. So what about the next race? The next class? Thirty euros here, fifty euros there?

## Scene 25

To be fair, right now Blizzard says: no battle pass, no Wow Token, no paid boosts. Right now. But a paid race on day one is a precedent. On top of a subscription.

## Scene 26

And I'll make a prediction. I know Blizzard. With these seasons, they're going to make a ton of money. Every new race will be paid. Every new class too, probably. Maybe free after a year or so. But let's not kid ourselves: a game like this, with no expansions, will not live without paid content. I don't believe it for a second. It would be sad to see World of Warcraft go that way.

## Scene 27

And let's talk about that paid race. I actually like the Skyborne idea. Air elves, the lore of Eldre'Thalas, the Highborne. I love it. But the design? I checked the game files. The Skyborne are built on the blood elf skeleton: same bones, same animations, same silhouette. And for armor, textures and helmets, the game falls back on the blood elf. Exactly like the void elves in retail. New paint, same body. It's a blue blood elf. A high elf, recolored.

## Scene 28

Blizzard is the champion of recycling, I know. But at least take the Nightborne models from retail and rework them into something new. Thirty dollars for a recolor is hard to swallow.

## Scene 29

Raids in December, Hardcore in winter, a big update in spring, another one in summer. A huge endgame block every three months or so. That feels fast. Very fast. Some players need that rhythm, I get it. But instead of giant blocks, why not smaller, really interesting content in between?

## Scene 30

Since it's not Classic, take the best of twenty years of World of Warcraft. Some past expansions had great gameplay ideas. Push class identity, and make every spec feel unique. Without losing the Classic logic I love: you can't make a ghost or a machine bleed. That's Warcraft. For me, Forever should have been World of Warcraft 2.

## Scene 31

Warcraft was born from war. Power struggles, ego wars, hatred between races. A dark fantasy world.

## Scene 32

Look at retail right now: Lady Liadrin dies in patch 12.1.5, and players are furious. Not because she died, but because it was rushed, with no weight. Most of the veteran developers are gone, and the new vision is different from the players who built this game.

## Scene 33

Remember the Worldsoul Saga announcement? Chris Metzen was back, and everyone thought he would save World of Warcraft. In my opinion, he was brought back for his name and his image. I don't see his touch in the game today. Yet look at his tabletop games at Warchief Gaming: that's his style, clearly.

## Scene 34

And then Holly Longdale says that Metzen sometimes wishes they hadn't called it Warcraft, because it sounds intimidating. Really? The man who helped write Garrosh Hellscream, one of the most brutal warlords in Warcraft? I find that very hard to believe. Unless he said it in a sarcastic way.

## Scene 35

The problem is that everything became political. I'm not afraid to say it: Blizzard needs a reset. A neutral creative direction, built on what Warcraft is. Not everyone in this world is a big tough warrior, and that's fine, it takes everyone to make a world. But if some people don't like the identity of Warcraft, they can make another game, for that audience. Overwatch is a good example: an original title that fits that other audience much better. And I don't mind, Overwatch is Overwatch. But Warcraft must stay Warcraft. It must not become Disney. And I can feel that softer tone slowly coming into Forever too.

## Scene 36

Let's go further. With Blizzard's resources, couldn't they have gone much further in rebuilding Azeroth? This game deserved a new graphics engine. Way more new content. New quests, new stories, everywhere. The new dungeons are cool, yes. But honestly? We're settling for crumbs.

## Scene 37

If we're realistic, Forever should be delayed by a year, to be rebuilt properly. But retail is in such a bad place, and we're so nostalgic for the old World of Warcraft, that we accept crumbs. That's where we are. It's sad to say. And I know it's too late. It will never be delayed.

## Scene 38

But I know what would have worked for everyone. Even the people who think they wouldn't like it on paper would have loved it in game: A completely new game. Old school mechanics. Rooted in the lore. Less "you are the hero, the chosen legend". More adventure in the world.

## Scene 39

Because the key of World of Warcraft is the world. And the conflict between the Horde and the Alliance. Stop making them best friends every time a new threat shows up. Sometimes a threat is huge for one faction and minor for the other. And that's fine. That's war. That's Warcraft.

## Scene 40

Forever is not a bad game. But Blizzard can do so much better, if they gave themselves the means. They won't. They'll do the bare minimum for maximum profit. Let's not kid ourselves.

## Scene 41

And here's the thing people don't realize. I think the future of World of Warcraft, as a franchise, depends on Forever more than anything else. Not life or death. But what Blizzard decides to do with World of Warcraft. And I see three scenarios.

## Scene 42

Scenario one: huge hype at launch, then it runs out of breath fast. A bit like Classic did. Then Blizzard won't bother. They'll keep the usual formula. The retail formula.

## Scene 43

Scenario two: Forever holds strong, all the way, for two years. Then I think Forever becomes the main World of Warcraft.

## Scene 44

Scenario three: players love a lot of what Forever does, but the "it must stay Classic" side holds back a lot of people. Then I think Blizzard makes a new game. The sequel to retail, taking all the good ideas from Forever, in a modern way. A World of Warcraft 2.

## Scene 45

From a pure marketing point of view, that's exactly what they're measuring right now. Forever is a test.

## Scene 46

And retail? Honestly, I wouldn't be surprised if it ends like this. Xal'atath fails to merge with Azeroth, so someone else does it instead. Out of nowhere. Azshara comes back. Maybe Illidan, who knows. Nothing shocks me anymore. Then the Titans show up, unhappy, because Azeroth's energy was supposed to feed their plans. Big scandal, a little fight. And in the end, we free Sargeras, because we "understand his point of view". Plot twist: the villain wasn't the villain. Very original, as usual. Everybody merges with Azeroth, the energy is shared with everyone, and everything goes back to normal.

## Scene 47

And if there's a World of Warcraft 2, I bet it takes place thirty or forty years later. Don't look any further. That's how it's going to be.

## Scene 48

So Blizzard, be clear. It's not Classic. It will never be Classic. It's a remake, a new start. Sell it like that, and build it like that.

## Scene 50

Am I wrong about all this? Tell me. And follow for every World of Warcraft Forever update.
